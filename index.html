      Terms & Conditions

      Effective 2026-08-10 · Last updated 2026-08-10

      1. What ErrorLens is

      ErrorLens is a local-first security scanning tool that runs entirely in your browser. It provides guided checklists, smoke probes, and report compilation to help you review web3 apps, APIs, mobile apps, and LLM features. It is not a hosted service and stores no data on our servers.

      2. Authorized use only

      Only run scans against targets you own or have explicit written authorization to test. Never use the tool to attack production systems, bypass rate limits, exfiltrate data, drain funds, or execute destructive transactions. ErrorLens is a verification aid — findings should be confirmed with fork, read-only, or simulated evidence before any claim is made.

      3. Local data & privacy

      Scan state, findings, and settings are stored in your browser's localStorage. There is no account, no telemetry, and no third-party analytics. Network requests are made only when you run smoke checks, and only to the target URL, RPC endpoint, or CORS proxy you configure. Optional AI drafting sends the finding title, severity, surface, and area to the OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key you provide — only when you click "Draft with AI".

      4. No warranty

      ErrorLens is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. Checklists and automated probes are guidance, not proof. A clean scan does not guarantee a target is secure, and ErrorLens is not a substitute for a professional security audit by qualified reviewers.

      5. Limitation of liability

      To the maximum extent permitted by law, ErrorLens and its contributors are not liable for any damages arising from your use of the tool, including losses caused by findings, missed findings, or actions taken in response to a report.

      Privacy Policy

      Effective 2026-08-10 · Last updated 2026-08-10

      What we collect

      Nothing. ErrorLens is a static site with no backend. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data, and we do not use cookies, trackers, or analytics scripts.

      Where your data lives

      Everything you enter — scan scope, findings, custom checklists, and settings — is written to your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your machine. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

      Network requests you control

      Running smoke checks issues requests only to (a) the target site URL you provide, (b) the RPC endpoint you provide, or (c) a CORS proxy you configure to read security headers. We do not proxy your traffic through our own servers.

      AI-assisted drafting (optional)

      If you enable it, the API key and endpoint you enter are stored only in your browser. When you click "Draft with AI", the current finding's title, severity, surface, and area are sent to that endpoint. We do not see or store these requests.

      Exports

      Reports and session files you download or upload are yours. We never access them. Session exports deliberately exclude your AI key.
