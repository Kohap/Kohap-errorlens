Operating rules

    Persona guide

    Founder vs Researcher

    Founder mode keeps every hint in plain language. You can describe what your product does, pick the surfaces you own, and hand your developers a report they can act on — without running any exploits yourself.

    Researcher mode shows the technical playbook: attack questions, evidence capture, fork-prove discipline, and honest severity. Log confirmed findings only — report hardening gaps and test gaps separately.
